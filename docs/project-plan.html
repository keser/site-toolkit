Recent Activity

What changed since the last snapshot (2026-09-18) — one design session and one on-site change.

  • SE-corner hedge removed (2026-09-29): the street-side hedge is gone and the ground raked level. No planting decision yet — windbreak/screening options still open.
  • Compost designs finalized (2026-09-27): the hot compost (S1) becomes a 3–4 bin turning system that also takes the coop manure/bedding, with a hardware-cloth-lined, lidded fresh-fill bay against tunneling. The cold compost (S2) stays a single open corral — the earlier “3-bin” label belonged to S1.
  • Wood processing formalized: a two-section, elevated chip-staging area (“drying” / “ready to chip”) in the windy NW corner (R4), and a sorted, skid-mounted wood pile (R19).
  • New task D6 — pawpaw understory: pawpaw and a future mushroom bed move to the camper side of Zone 5, freeing the mulberry/pine corner for the compost/wood-processing cluster.
