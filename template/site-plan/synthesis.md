become). Fill this in once the profile sections have their findings, and before any design
decisions.

## Open questions before design

Anything the profile flagged as unverified, estimated, or contradictory: unconfirmed local rules,
unmeasured flows, estimated heights, observations that disagree with the data. Each one needs an
answer, or a decision to proceed without one, before design starts.
