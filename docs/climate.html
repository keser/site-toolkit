Monthly temperature and precipitation normals, USDA hardiness zone, and wind summary digitized from the site's Lesson 1 Climate Survey (Figma Slides, June 2026), sourced there via WeatherSpark and NASA POWER. Record high/low and elevation via NOAA (Hartford-Brainard Airport / Hartford, ~18 mi from site) and the USGS National Elevation Dataset. The hardiness zone map is the USDA's 2023 map (credited under it); the bioregion map is still a placeholder. The annual wind rose's percentages are approximate — reconstructed from the source Figma chart's wedge geometry, not re-queried from underlying station data — and kept here for fidelity to the original Lesson 1 assignment. The four seasonal wind roses below it are the actual data: 10 years of hourly METAR observations from Hartford-Brainard Airport (KHFD, ~16 km from the site), reused directly from the Lesson 3 Sector Analysis report. They resolve what the annual chart averages away — winter prevails from the N, while spring, summer, and fall all trend S (summer most strongly, at 23.7%). Wind data: Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS archive for station HFD (Hartford-Brainard Airport), via mesonet.agron.iastate.edu — real hourly wind observations, not a forecast model. Seasons are meteorological (Winter = Dec/Jan/Feb, etc). Direction bins are 16-point compass (22.5° sectors).
