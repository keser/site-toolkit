Findings for design

What this part of the profile means for a design, sorted as strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. The top findings roll up into the site-wide synthesis.

Strengths

  • 74 species logged on site, each with a GPS-located photo.
  • Natives already thriving include northern highbush blueberry, riverbank grape, eastern black walnut, and wild bergamot.

Weaknesses

  • The earlier plant list has more introduced species (34) than native ones (27), including invasives such as Asian bittersweet, tree-of-heaven, Norway maple, Japanese stiltgrass, and purple loosestrife.

Opportunities

  • The natives already established show what the site will support.
  • The river corridor means new native habitat matters beyond the lot.

Threats

  • Bittersweet and Virginia creeper are already damaging the back fence.
  • Invasives keep spreading without active control.
