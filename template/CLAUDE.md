## Privacy
Private repo, private by default. Neighbors and other third parties are never named; refer to
them by role. Images, PDFs, parcel records, and survey scans stay out of git (see `.gitignore`);
keep them in a private folder and link by path. Track a finished asset deliberately with
`git add -f`. Never write utility account numbers.

## Tasks and phases
Phases are defined per site in `site-plan/phases.md`. Tasks use IDs prefixed by phase and may
depend across phases. The detail and complexity of tasks is up to the site.
`site-plan/progress-log.md` is the dated narrative (newest first): what happened and why the plan changed.
